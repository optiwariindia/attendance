# Daily Progress Report - May 14, 2026 (Evening Update)

## Overview
Today marked a major milestone in transition from basic data capture to intelligent workforce reporting. We successfully launched a dynamic attendance reporting engine and established the foundational data layer for immutable leave management.

## Key Achievements

### 1. Intelligent Attendance Reporting Engine
Developed a robust reporting system that provides deep insights into employee presence and performance:
- **Status Sophistication:** Introduced context-aware statuses including **"In Office"** (live green indicator), **"Awaited"** (pending for today), and **"Absent"** (past dates with missing records).
- **Timezone & Precision Fixes:** Standardized local date component formatting to eliminate UTC shifts and implemented heuristics to handle legacy millisecond data, ensuring accurate break and net-work calculations.
- **Future Visibility:** The dashboard now projects future **Holidays** and **Weekly Offs**, allowing employees to view their upcoming schedule before attendance records are even generated.
- **Comp-Off Identification:** Implemented automatic tagging of punches on off-days as potential "Comp-Off Candidates".

### 2. Immutable Attendance Snapshots
Enhanced system integrity by ensuring historical accuracy:
- **Unified Day Context:** Attendance records now store a snapshot of the day's context (Holiday/Weekly-Off/Leave) at the time of creation.
- **Automated Population:** The Midnight Routine was updated to automatically identify and lock in this context during daily entry generation.

### 3. Leave Management Foundation
Initiated Phase 2 of the roadmap with a focus on data integrity and balance tracking:
- **LeaveBalance Model:** Implemented annual quota tracking with audit trails for manual adjustments.
- **LeaveApplication (Snapshot-Based):** Designed the application model to store an immutable copy of the `LeaveCategory` settings at the time of request, ensuring that historical records remain consistent even if policies change.

### 4. Administrative Flexibility
- **Configurable Weekly Offs:** Upgraded the User model and Admin UI to support flexible rest days (multi-select), moving away from a hardcoded Sunday default.
