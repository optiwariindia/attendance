# Landing Page Content & Structure - Mini HRMS

This document outlines the design and content strategy for the Mini HRMS public landing page.

## 1. Hero Section (The Hook)
- **Headline:** Empower Your Fieldwork Teams with GPS-Verified Precision.
- **Sub-headline:** The only SaaS HRMS designed specifically for research fieldwork solutions. Track attendance, manage leaves, and verify field presence from anywhere.
- **Primary CTA:** [Start Your Free Trial] - (Links to `/register-tenant`)
- **Secondary CTA:** [Watch Demo]
- **Visual:** Mockup of the Mobile App showing a GPS Clock-in event.

## 2. Key Value Propositions
### A. Verification Anywhere
- **Content:** No more "proxy" attendance. Every clock-in is verified with high-accuracy GPS coordinates and server-side timestamps.
### B. SaaS Simplicity (Multi-tenancy)
- **Content:** Register your company in minutes. No server setup required. Manage multiple branches and departments from a single dashboard.
### C. Employee Self-Service (ESS)
- **Content:** Provide value to your researchers with instant access to appointment letters, appraisals, and salary slips directly on their phones.

## 3. How It Works (The 3-Step Process)
1.  **Self-Register:** Fill out our simple onboarding form to create your company instance.
2.  **Configure:** Define your branches, departments, and shift policies in the setup wizard.
3.  **Onboard:** Invite your team. They complete their profiles and start clocking in immediately.

## 4. Feature Highlights
- **Geofencing:** Restrict attendance to specific field site radiuses.
- **Holiday-Aware Leaves:** Automated duration calculations that respect your company's holiday calendar.
- **Check-Marks Audit:** A professional verification layer for administrators to audit and approve attendance logs.
- **Capacitor Mobile App:** Native Android & iOS support for seamless field use.

## 5. Trust & Social Proof
- **Section:** Trusted by Leading Research Organizations (Placeholder for logos).
- **Testimonial:** "This system transformed how we track our field teams in remote areas." - Lead Researcher.

## 6. Footer
- **Links:** Features, Pricing, About Us, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service.
- **Contact:** Sales & Support information.
- **Branding:** Developed for Frequent Research Fieldwork Solutions.
