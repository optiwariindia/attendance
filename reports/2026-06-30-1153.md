## Decisions
- Chose to patch `node_modules` automatically via a lightweight `postinstall` script. Since we updated `package.json`, Docker's cache for `RUN npm install` will be invalidated on the next build, forcing a fresh install where the patch is automatically applied. This permanently and cleanly resolves the warning at its source without hiding other potential Node.js warnings.
